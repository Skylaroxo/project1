Case Study · Event Curation

Shanghai Football Only

A one-day football-themed fan art expo that a friend and I co-curated and co-funded. It brought 400 attendees and 82 exhibitors together, and every ticket sold out within 10 days.

The Challenge

Football fans who make fan art, zines and handmade goods had few events made just for them. My friend and I wanted to create a space where they could sell their work and meet each other in person. We planned the whole event ourselves and paid for it with our own money, so every decision about tickets, booths and costs mattered.

The Solution

We planned a one-day "ONLY" expo focused on football fan creations. Together we recruited exhibitors, opened ticket sales, and promoted the event on Weibo and Xiaohongshu. I also handled everything from the visual materials to the venue logistics, and led a team of 19 staff members on the day.

Results & Key Outcomes

400

tickets sold out in 10 days

82

exhibitors

500+

participants on the day

The expo received positive engagement on Weibo and Xiaohongshu after the event, and earned a strong return through ticket sales, merchandise sales and booth revenue.
